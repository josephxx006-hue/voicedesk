Questions, answered.

Do I need a phone number to start?

No. The prototype works without a phone number. A business number would only be needed later when we connect real WhatsApp or phone-call features.

Can the owner take over?

Yes. That's a core part of the design: the AI should not pretend to know something it doesn't.

Will this work on a phone?

Yes. The interface is designed mobile-first, and the dashboard can later become a full mobile app.
